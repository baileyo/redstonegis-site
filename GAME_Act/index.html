        What each side says

        • Out of Bounds Ranch says most of the federal land sits in an area the BLM has listed as having significantly restricted access, and that the Roan parcels would connect about 8,900 acres of BLM land to the north with a larger block to the south.
        • Colorado Parks and Wildlife took no formal position in its September 3, 2026 letter. It wrote that the federal parcel holds elk calving areas and severe winter range, that losing it would limit access to the national forest to the north, and that the Roan parcels open no BLM land that was previously landlocked.
        • Garfield County commissioners backed the exchange 2-0 on July 23, 2026 and asked that the Glenwood-side land stay undeveloped.
        • Wilderness Workshop opposes the bill and calls it a bad deal for western Colorado.

        Reporting through mid-November 2026 describes the bill as drafted by Rep. Jeff Hurd and not yet introduced.

        How this was mapped, and its limits
        • The BLM Colorado State Office map dated February 23, 2026 was georeferenced by matching its BLM-land pattern to the national Surface Management Agency layer. Parcel edges then follow that layer's BLM boundary wherever the map shows them coinciding.
        • The line between Timber Valley and Cow Creek, the west edge of Timber Valley and the east edge of Cow Creek are traced from the map image and are good to roughly 100 feet at best.
        • The Out of Bounds Ranch outline covers only the ranch parcels drawn on the BLM map inset, about 2,700 acres. News reports put the ranch near 4,000 acres.
        • The ranch says it now proposes to leave out as much BLM land south of County Road 115 as it can. Its revised Fischer Creek map shows the southwest corner staying BLM; that corner is drawn separately here. Final boundaries would be set by survey.
        • Shaded relief is computed from USGS 3DEP elevation: 30-meter data for the region and 10-meter data around each exchange area.
        • NAIP imagery covers only the two exchange areas, at about 4 meters per pixel, taken from the USGS National Map NAIP service in November 2026. The service does not report the flight year for these tiles.
        • Land ownership is PAD-US 4.1; roads and water are Census TIGER; hunting units are CPW game management units.
