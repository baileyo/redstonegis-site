Wallingford, Vermont: original lots

Lots of three divisions as drawn in 1795: a strip of 30 lots along Otter Creek, a north-west block, and the east and middle blocks. The block name is part of each label because lot numbers repeat.

Lots and names: Copy of Wallingford's old "Parchment Plan" (John Hilburn Jr., surveyor, November 1795), made for the selectmen, July 1919 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
