Stoddard: proprietors' lots, 1753–1772

Monadnock No. 7. Each of 130 shares drew three lots. Model grid, not a survey. Hover a lot; its share's other lots light up.

    Hover or tap a lot

    Draft of Lots and plan, 2 Jan 1753; Schedule of Proprietors and Lots, 9 Oct 1767; settlement reports of 1770 and 1771; re-entry of 15 June 1772. New Hampshire State Papers vol. 28, pp. 268–296. The grid is drawn from the charter's dimensions and fitted to the present town line (dashed, Census 2024); lot positions are approximate. Dark cells are in the grid but not in the schedule.
