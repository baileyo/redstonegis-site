About this map

Each outline is a dated version of a state, territory or other area from the Atlas of Historical County Boundaries, John H. Long, editor (Chicago: The Newberry Library, 2010): 220 outlines, beginning with the Treaty of Paris on 3 September 1783.

The map shows the outlines in force on 31 December of the selected year. An area is marked for five years after it was created or its boundary changed. Selecting an area lists its whole history, for example Alaska as a department, a district, a territory and a state.

Every state and territory in view is labelled: with its name where that fits inside the outline, otherwise with the atlas's short name, set outside with a leader line where there is no room.

Other covers the Vermont and Texas republics, Deseret, the Oregon Country and an uncertain boundary area. The atlas ends in 2000; no state boundary is changed after that here. Outlines are simplified to about 400 metres for the web. Alaska and Hawaii are drawn as insets, Alaska at about a third of its true size.
