Henniker: draft of lots, 1753

Number Six in the line of towns. Eighty draughts of three lots each, drawn at Portsmouth 11 July 1753. Model grid, not a survey. Hover a lot; the other lots of its draught light up.

    Hover or tap a lot

    Draft of Lots in Henniker, 11 July 1753, and the plan of 12 June 1753 by Matthew Patten and Daniel Leslie, New Hampshire State Papers vol. 27, pp. 330–332. Ranges are half-mile strips running east and west, numbered here 1 at the south to 13 at the north; lots are numbered from the east. The grid is fitted to the present town line (dashed, Census 2024); lot positions are approximate. Masonian proprietors are tagged by surname.
