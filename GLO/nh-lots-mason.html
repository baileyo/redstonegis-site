Mason: draft of lots, 1749

Township No. 1. Sixty-eight draughts of three lots each, 50 for the grantees and 18 for the Masonian proprietors. Model grid, not a survey. Hover a lot; the other lots of its draught light up.

    Hover or tap a lot

    Draft of Lots in Mason, drawn at Dunstable 16 Oct 1749, and the plan of Mason, New Hampshire State Papers vol. 27, pp. 472–474. The grid is drawn from the dimensions in the charter and fitted to present Mason and Greenville (dashed, Census 2024); lot positions are approximate.
