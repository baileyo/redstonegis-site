Cabot, Vermont: original lots and proprietors

72 lots, numbered up and down the columns from the south-east corner. Each lot shows the name written in it; two lots carry none.

Lots and names: "Plan of lands in Cabot and part of Marshfield", traced from the original by E. Gilman; redrafted 1970 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
