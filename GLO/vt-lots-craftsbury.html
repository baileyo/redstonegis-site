Craftsbury, Vermont: town lots and original proprietors

144 half-mile lots, chartered 1781 as Minden. Each lot shows the name written in it on the plan. Lots are labelled number-range (column across the Albany line, row down the Greensboro line).

Lots and names: "Craftsbury: Town Lots and Original Proprietors", tracing by B. Barton, corrected by Burns, 1968 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
