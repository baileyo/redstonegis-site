Calais, Vermont: original lots

Three divisions of 65 lots each (160, 112 and 77 acres), surveyed in 1783. Labels give lot and division (d1 to d3).

Lots and names: "Lotting Plan, Town of Calais, Vt.", E. H. Palmer, 1927 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
