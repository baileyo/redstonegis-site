Walden, Vermont: original lots and proprietors

216 lots: 72 numbered tiers of three, one lot in each of the first, second and third divisions. Labels are tier number and division. Each lot shows the name written in it.

Lots and names: "Lot Map of Walden, Vermont", copied from town records by Paul M. Reed, September 1945 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
