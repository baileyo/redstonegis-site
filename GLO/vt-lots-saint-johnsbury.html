St. Johnsbury, Vermont: lots of the 1787 survey

71 numbered lots and about twenty smaller pieces. Each lot shows the name written in it; the pieces along the rivers carry the names of early settlers.

Lots and names: "Town Plat of St. Johnsbury, Vermont, surveyed in 1787", traced from E. G. Humphrey's plan of 1878 by J.M.P.; traced again by Paul M. Reed, 1945 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
