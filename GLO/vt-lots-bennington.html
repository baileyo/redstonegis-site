Bennington, Vermont: lotting plan of the 1749 grant

64 lots of about 360 acres, numbered from the south-west corner. The plan names no holders.

Lots and names: "Lotting Plan of the Township of Bennington, granted January 8, 1749", redrafted by M. E. Raboin, 1972 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
