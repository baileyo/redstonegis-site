Lots and names: "A Plan of Roxbury, after plan in Town Clerk's office with corrections from actual surveys", R.J.F., 1910 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
