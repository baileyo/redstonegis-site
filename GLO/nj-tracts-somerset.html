East Jersey Proprietary Tracts

Somerset County north of the Raritan River: tracts sold by the East Jersey Proprietors, 1683–1744, from the tract map and list in Snell's 1881 county history (copied from the 1747 Elizabethtown Bill in Chancery). Positions are good to roughly 300–500 m.
