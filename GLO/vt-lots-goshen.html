Goshen, Vermont: original lots and proprietors (Crafts plan)

70 lots of about 208 acres, each with the name written in it. The plan is laid on the ground from the town's west line only.

Lots and names: "Lotting Plan of Goshen", a copy by Mike Kerin of the map in the Samuel Crafts Book of Plans, Secretary of State's office (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
