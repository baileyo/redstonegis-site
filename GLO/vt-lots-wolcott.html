Wolcott, Vermont: original lots

Large lots in a west and an east block, a strip of 38 lots along Craftsbury, a strip of 24 down the middle, and three rows of lots across the south.

Lots and names: Wolcott lotting plan, copied 1915 from a map in the town clerk's office (E. R. Welch); recopied 1968, traced 1980 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
