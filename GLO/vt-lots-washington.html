Washington, Vermont: original lots and proprietors

Ten ranges of 16 lots, two ranges of 18, and 18 gore lots along the Williamstown line. Each lot shows the name written in it.

Hover or tap a lot. Type a name to highlight every lot it holds.

Lots and names: "Town of Washington", copied from the plan and field book of Thomas Brown (survey of 1784-85) by Chester Dickey, 1907; traced 1941 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
