Lots and names: "Groton and Harris Gore", compiled 1970 by the Vermont Department of Forests and Parks from charters, Crafts plans and lot plans (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
