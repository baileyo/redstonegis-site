Braintree, Vermont: original lots

Second division (four tiers across the north), first division (seven tiers) and what is left of the third division after the 1824 annexation to Rochester. Labels give lot and division (d1 to d3).

Lots and names: "Braintree", copy of the original plan of 1801 (Isaac Nichols Jr., proprietors' clerk), F. H. Dewart, 1920; traced 1945 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
