Lots and names: "Township of Hardwick, Vt.", from the original map of May 16, 1788 by Delphus Walbridge; copy by Paul M. Reed (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
