Glover, Vermont: original lots and proprietors

144 half-mile lots, chartered 1783. Each lot shows the name the plan's key gives for it.

Hover or tap a lot. Type a name to highlight every lot it holds.

Lots and names: "Town Lotting Plan of Glover, Vermont", from the Crafts Book of Plans, drawn by M. Kerin, 1970 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
