Bergen Patents from the 1764 Field Book

79 tracts in the old Township of Bergen (now Jersey City and its neighbours in Hudson County), drawn from the courses and distances the commissioners recorded in 1764. Each is set at its number on L. F. Douglass's 1841 map or its inset of the Town of Bergen, or hung from the corner of a neighbour that is. The shapes are measured; the positions are approximate: town lots to a few tens of metres, the larger tracts to about 200 metres, and a long strip may sit too far along its own length.

Sources: Charles H. Winfield, History of the Land Titles in Hudson County, N.J. (1872), which prints the Field Book of 1764, read from the Internet Archive's machine text; L. F. Douglass, Topographical map of Jersey City, Hoboken, and the adjacent country (1841), Library of Congress. Holders and dates are a first machine reading. A name or year followed by a question mark could not be tied to its patent heading and may belong to a different patent. Municipal lines are modern, from the Census Bureau. Only tracts whose survey closes and that could be tied to a number on the map are shown.
