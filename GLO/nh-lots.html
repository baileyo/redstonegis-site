New Hampshire proprietors’ lots

New Hampshire granted its land a township at a time. The grantees, called proprietors, then surveyed each township into lots and drew for them. These three townships are drawn lot by lot from the proprietors’ own lists.

  • Stoddard, 1753–1772

    390 lots in 130 shares. The draft of 1753, the schedule of 1767, and which lots were settled, improved or forfeited by 1770–72.

    Masonian grant of 1752 · Cheshire County

  • Mason, 1749

    68 draughts over twenty ranges of ten lots, with a gore of thirteen lots on the Massachusetts line.

    Masonian grant of 1749 · Hillsborough County

  • Henniker, 1753

    80 draughts of three lots each, over thirteen ranges of nineteen lots.

    Masonian regrant of a Massachusetts township · Merrimack County

These are model grids, not surveys. The numbering of ranges and lots is read from the plan printed with each draw list, and the grid is fitted to the present town line, so a lot’s position on the ground is approximate. Each page says what was checked and what does not fit. Draw lists and plans: Provincial and State Papers of New Hampshire, vols. XXVII–XXVIII (Masonian Papers), read from the page images. Town outlines: U.S. Census Bureau, 2024.
