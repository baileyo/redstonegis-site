Vershire, Vermont: lots and first proprietors (1783 plan)

About 220 lots in 13 columns, three divisions mixed together. Each lot shows its number, its division (I, II or III) and the name written in it.

Lots and names: "Plan of the Town of Vershire, Vermont, as it was laid out and divided in the year 1783; with the names of the 1st proprietors on their respective lots"; copied 1924 by F. H. Dewart, traced 1946 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
