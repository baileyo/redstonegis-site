Richford, Vermont: lots of the 1822-23 survey

Lots of four divisions on a grid of about 13 columns and 19 rows. Each grid cell shows the lot number, its division (d1 to d4) and the name or names written in it.

Hover or tap a lot. Type a name to highlight every lot it holds.

Lots and names: "A Correct Plan of the Town of Richford from actual survey in 1822 and 3 by Joseph Beeman"; copy by F. H. Dewart, 1900; traced by the Vermont Forest Service, 1954 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
