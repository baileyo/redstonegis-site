Lots and names: "Map of Springfield showing divisions, ranges and lots as drawn by the original proprietors", a small printed diagram from a town history (page 7) (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
