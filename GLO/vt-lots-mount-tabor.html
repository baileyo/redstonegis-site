Mount Tabor, Vermont: original lots

Twelve ranges of lots 6 to 12, a block of ranges 1 to 6 (lots 3 to 5), and thirty numbered lots in the north-east. The Creek Tier along Otter Creek was never lotted on this plan.

Lots and names: "Mount Tabor, Vt.", 1904, scale 80 rods to the inch (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
