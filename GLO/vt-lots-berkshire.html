Berkshire, Vermont: original lots and proprietors

Three sets of lots: 76 in the west, 57 down the middle and 66 larger lots in the east. Each lot shows the name written in it.

Lots and names: "Town Lots of Berkshire", David Fay, surveyor; copy of the original by A. Hull and L. Royce, August 1842; recopied December 1968 by N. S. Morgan (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
