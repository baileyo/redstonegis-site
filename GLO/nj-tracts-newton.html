West Jersey Original Surveys

Newton Township in the Third (Irish) Tenth, now Camden, Collingswood, Haddon Township and neighbours: the 23 original surveys of 1681–c.1700, from John Clement's 1877 map and key, with the owners' names Thomas Sharp wrote on his 1700 map. The source is a relative-position sketch stretched to fit modern creeks, so boundaries are approximate (roughly 300–600 m).
