New Jersey proprietors' tracts

Three places drawn tract by tract: 143 tracts in all

The main map places each New Jersey first title in the square of the creek, town or county its record names. These pages go one step down, to the tracts themselves, for three places where an old map or a book of surveys made that possible. Each was built from a different source, so each has its own accuracy, given on its page.

How to read them. The shapes are drawn from old maps and surveys and fitted to present-day rivers, streets and town lines. They are approximate: from a few tens of metres for the Bergen town lots to several hundred metres for the larger tracts elsewhere. Names are as the sources give them.
