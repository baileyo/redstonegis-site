Andover, Vermont: original lots

Thirteen ranges of 16 to 26 lots and a gore of 15 lots on the west, as laid out 1763 to 1779. The plan covers old Andover, which included Weston until 1799.

Lots and names: "Andover", true copy of the original town clerk's plan (William Russell, 1779), F. H. Dewart, 1920 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
