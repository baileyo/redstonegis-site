Lots and names: Plan of Newark, H. H. Hobson, surveyor, 1907 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more.
