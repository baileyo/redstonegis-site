Lots and names: "Plan of Town of Worcester, Vt." (copy with pencilled names taken in 1960 from a similar map held in Worcester) (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
