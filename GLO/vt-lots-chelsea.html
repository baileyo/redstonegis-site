Chelsea, Vermont: original lots and proprietors

Two sets of 76 lots: large lots across the south and east, and small lots along the Washington line and down the Brookfield side. Each lot shows the name written in it.

Lots and names: "Chelsea, County of Orange, Vermont", traced from the Proprietors Plan by Fred E. Dwinell, August 1945 (Vermont State Archives, via VCGI). The grid is fitted to the present town outline (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023; white line), not surveyed; a lot line can be off by tens of metres or more. A name is the proprietor the plan assigns the lot to, not necessarily a settler.
